Mid-month budget alerts: catch spending leaks before month-end

2026-09-15

Month-end reports arrive too late. Use a mid-month Sheet check (and category focus) to spot dining, shopping, and subscription leaks while you can still fix them.

Chooser checklists in 2026 keep repeating: a tracker’s job is the warning mid-month, not only a pretty chart after salary day.

A simple mid-month ritual (15th)

  1. Open this month’s Sheet tab.
  2. Sum Food + Shopping + Subscriptions.
  3. If any line is already >50% of your informal monthly cap, cut that channel for two weeks.
  4. Skim small UPI taps - chai, delivery, recharges - they hide leaks.

Automation vs attention

SMS capture reduces typing. The decision still needs a human glance. Pair auto-capture with today / reports so the Sheet is ready when you check.
