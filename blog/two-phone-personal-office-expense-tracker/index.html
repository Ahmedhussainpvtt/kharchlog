Personal vs office UPI: one Expense tracker for two phones

2026-09-16

Many Indians keep a work SIM and a personal UPI app. How an Expense tracker can still produce one honest Google Sheet without mixing office reimbursements into household kharch.

A second Android phone for office WhatsApp is common. So is a work UPI ID on PhonePe and a personal GPay. If you only glance at one app's insights, household money looks healthier than it is. You need one Expense tracker that can tell office spend from home spend.

What actually gets mixed

  • Office cab and meal UPI on the personal UPI ID
  • Personal kirana on the work SIM because that phone was in the bag
  • Reimbursements that look like income until you tag them

A simple split that survives real weeks

  1. Keep one Google Sheet as the system of record.
  2. Use two categories or a payee prefix: Office: vs household names.
  3. Let bank SMS feed drafts, then sort in needs-review before they stick.

Why SMS still helps with two phones

Each phone only sees the SMS it receives. If salary and UPI alerts land on the personal SIM, install Kharch Log there. Office-only alerts on the work phone can stay out of the household Expense tracker, or you log reimbursements manually once they hit your account.

Quick FAQ

Do I need two Kharch Log accounts? No. One Google account and one Sheet is enough if you tag office rows. Two Sheets also work if you prefer a clean office export for HR.
