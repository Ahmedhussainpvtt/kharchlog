Google Sheets expense tracker vs closed budget apps

2026-08-26

Apps like Monarch and Goodbudget are excellent all-in-one money hubs. The tradeoff is often that your history lives inside their product. A Google Sheets expense tracker keeps every row under your Google account.

When Sheets wins

  • You want CSV/Excel export forever
  • You share one ledger with a partner via Drive
  • You already think in spreadsheets for taxes

How Kharch Log fits

Use the Android app for speed (manual, voice, or SMS). Keep Sheets as the system of record — similar spirit to “printable trackers,” but live and searchable.
