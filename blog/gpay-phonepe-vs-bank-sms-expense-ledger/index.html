GPay / PhonePe history vs bank SMS: which expense ledger wins?

2026-09-13

App insights are handy, but multi-UPI households need one ledger. Why bank SMS + a Sheet beats exporting three payment-app histories every month.

Guides on “free UPI spend trackers” usually list BHIM, GPay, PhonePe, and a spreadsheet. That works - until you pay from three apps and one card.

What each source is good for

  • GPay / PhonePe insights - great if almost all spends sit in one app.
  • Bank / UPI SMS - one stream for every app and card the bank alerts on.
  • Your spreadsheet - the only place you fully control forever.

The practical winner for multi-app users

Treat bank SMS as the intake hose, then keep the permanent ledger in Google Sheets. That is how SMS expense tracking is supposed to work in India - without bank login or Account Aggregator consent for daily kharch.
