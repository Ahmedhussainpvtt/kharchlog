Google Play

We’re almost on the Play Store. We’d love you with us.

Kharch Log is in Google’s review. We expect it to go live in the next couple of days. Until then, the official APK on this site is the same app - signed by us, yours to install.

Download APK now Ask to join testers

If you have ever trusted a small tool with something as private as money, you already know how rare that is. We built Kharch Log so your kharch lives in your Google Sheet - not a vault we can lock. Getting onto Play is how we make that safer for people who will never sideload an APK. We’re working through it with care, not noise.

If you want to be a Play Store beta tester, we would be honoured. Testers are not “users we dump builds on.” You would be the people who tell us when an SMS from your bank looks wrong, when a screen feels unkind at 11pm, when a rupee goes missing. That kind of honesty is a gift. We will treat it that way.

You do not need to be a developer. You do not need spare hours every week. If you can install a test build, use it the way you already track money, and write back when something hurts or shines - that is enough. If you are a developer and want to contribute ideas, patches, or bank-SMS patterns from your city, write to us. We are a small team. A thoughtful note goes a long way.

We cannot promise everyone a tester slot on day one - Google caps closed testing lists - but we will answer, we will remember you, and we will add people as the list opens. No one is “too small” to help. The people who believed in us before the store badge are the ones we hope to still know after it.

How to join

  1. Email usUse the button above or the contact form. The email draft is ready: put your name and the Gmail you use on your Android phone (same as Play), then send.
  2. Tell us a littlePhone brand/Android version, and whether you use UPI, cash, or both. Optional: one bank whose SMS you care about. No passwords. Ever.
  3. We’ll replyWith a Play testing invite when a slot is ready, or we’ll keep you on the list. Meanwhile you can keep using the APK from kharchlog.com.

Thank you for even reading this far. Building for India, in public, is lonely work until someone like you shows up. If you only download the APK and never write - that still counts. If you write - we’ll read it with a full heart.
