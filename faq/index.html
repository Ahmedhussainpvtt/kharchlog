Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for install, privacy, SMS, Sheets, pricing, and Excel-style tracking.

How much will Kharch Log cost?

₹149 lifetime — one-time. Pay here, download the APK, sign in with the same Google email. No monthly fee.

Does it work with Google Sheets?

Yes. After Google Sign-In, the app creates a Kharch Log folder and spreadsheet in your Drive and syncs expenses and income there. See Sheets sync.

Can it read UPI and bank SMS?

Yes. Grant SMS permission and Kharch Log detects common Indian debit/credit messages. Unclear ones wait in the review inbox.

Do I need SMS permission?

Yes — SMS permission is required for bank and UPI auto-capture. That is how Kharch Log reads payment alerts on your phone.

Is my expense data private?

We don’t store your expense ledger on a Kharch Log server — entries sync directly to your Google Sheet. See the Privacy Policy.

When can I download the app?

Now. Get lifetime access, then download the APK and sign in with the same Google account you paid with.

Is Kharch Log a free expense tracker?

Download and try the APK; lifetime unlock is ₹149 one-time — no monthly fee. Built as a free-to-try daily expense tracker for Android in India with UPI SMS + Google Sheets.

Can I track daily kharch and personal spending?

Yes. Log personal kharch, cash, UPI, and income every day. SMS auto-capture fills gaps so small payments don’t vanish from memory.

Is this like an expense tracker Excel sheet?

Same ownership idea as a free excel expense tracker or income and expense tracker excel — but live in Google Sheets. Log on Android, sync to Drive, open as a sheet or export to Excel. See Sheets sync.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Android first. Your Google Sheet will still open on iPhone via the Sheets app if someone shares it with you.
