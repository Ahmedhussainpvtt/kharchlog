Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026 · Contact: kharchlog@gmail.com

1. Who we are

Kharch Log (“the App”) is a personal expense-tracking Android application. This policy explains what information the App uses and how it is handled.

2. Data we process

  • Google account email — used only to sign you in and connect to your Google Drive/Sheets.
  • Expense data you enter — amounts, categories, payment modes, comments, person name, salary keyword.
  • SMS content (for auto-capture) — SMS permission is required if you want the App to automatically capture bank/UPI transactions from messages on your device. The App reads relevant new messages locally to detect amounts and payees. We do not upload SMS to a Kharch Log server; matched details are written into your Google Sheet only. If you prefer not to grant SMS, you can still add expenses manually or by voice.
  • Google Drive / Sheets files created by the App — a folder named “Kharch Log” and a spreadsheet inside it. Your expense ledger is linked directly to this sheet under your Google account.

3. How Google access works

The App requests the limited Google Drive scope drive.file (“See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app”). It creates and manages its own Kharch Log folder/sheet. It is not intended to browse or modify unrelated files in your Drive.

4. Where data is stored

  • Expense rows are stored in your Google Sheet under your Google account — not on a Kharch Log server.
  • We do not operate a Kharch Log backend that collects, hosts, or sells your expense data. Sync goes from the App on your phone straight to Google Sheets/Drive.
  • Small local preferences (e.g. signed-in email, linked sheet id, profile fields) are stored on your device.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared with Google only as needed to provide Sign-In and Sheets/Drive features you enable. Anyone you share your Google Sheet with can see those rows.

6. Permissions

  • SMS — needed for automatic capture from bank/UPI messages. Without it, auto-capture is off; manual entry, voice add, reports, and Google Sheets sync still work. You can revoke SMS later in Android settings.
  • Battery / restricted settings — optional; helps SMS detection stay reliable on some phones.
  • Internet — required for Google Sign-In and sheet sync.
  • Microphone — only if you use voice add; processed for speech-to-text on device/OS services you already use.

7. Retention & deletion

Uninstalling the App removes local preferences on the device. Your Google Sheet and Drive folder remain in your Google account until you delete them. You can sign out in the App and revoke access anytime at Google Account → Third-party access.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

10. Contact

Questions: kharchlog@gmail.com
