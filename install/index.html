1. Install from Google Play

  1. Open Google PlaySearch for Kharch Log, or use the Play button on this page. Prefer paying at Easy Peeze pricing first with the Google email you'll use in the app.
  2. Tap InstallPlay downloads and installs the app. This site does not offer an APK.

2. Android 13+ "Restricted settings" (SMS)

After install, SMS permission may need one extra step on Android 13+:

  1. Open App info for Kharch LogLong-press the icon -> App info, or Settings -> Apps -> Kharch Log.
  2. Overflow menu, then Allow restricted settingsThen grant SMS when the app asks - needed for UPI / bank SMS capture.
