Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for install, privacy, SMS, and Sheets.

Is Kharch Log free?

Yes. When we launch, the core expense tracker will be free on Android. Data goes to your Google Sheet — not a paid vault on our servers.

Does it work with Google Sheets?

Yes. After Google Sign-In, the app creates a Kharch Log folder and spreadsheet in your Drive and syncs expenses and income there.

Can it read UPI and bank SMS?

Optionally. Grant SMS permission and Kharch Log detects common Indian debit/credit messages. Unclear ones wait in the review inbox.

Do I need SMS permission?

No. Manual entry, voice add, reports, and Sheets sync work without it. SMS is only for automatic capture.

Is my expense data private?

We don’t store your expense ledger on a Kharch Log backend. See the Privacy Policy for Google Sign-In and SMS details.

When can I download the app?

We are launching soon. There is no public download on this site yet — check back here for the release.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Android first. Your Google Sheet will still open on iPhone via the Sheets app if someone shares it with you.
