Your money book, on your phone

Kharch Log is an Android app that logs expenses and income — manually or from bank/UPI SMS — and saves them to a Google Sheet in your Google Drive.

Personal finance · Google Sign-In · Your data stays yours

What it does

  • Add expenses with amount, category, payment mode, and notes
  • Optional SMS auto-detect for common bank/UPI debit & credit messages
  • Creates a Kharch Log folder and spreadsheet in your Drive automatically
  • Uses limited Google Drive access: only files this app creates for you
