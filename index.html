Free expense tracker & personal finance logging

Kharch Log is an Android expense tracker for people who want a simple money book: log spending and income, auto-capture from bank/UPI SMS (SMS permission required), and keep every row in a Google Sheet you control. It sits between lightweight trackers (like Fast Budget / Lekhhaa) and spreadsheet ownership — without forcing enterprise expense-management complexity from tools such as Zoho Expense or Kredmint.

What you can track

  • Daily expenses and income (manual or voice)
  • UPI and bank SMS debits/credits when permission is granted
  • Categories, payment modes, payees, and notes
  • Today’s spend plus category reports
  • Shared household visibility via your Google Sheet

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers for install, privacy, SMS, Sheets, and pricing.

How much will Kharch Log cost?

₹149 lifetime — one-time access. No monthly fee. Will be launching soon.

Does it work with Google Sheets?

Yes. After Google Sign-In, the app creates a Kharch Log folder and spreadsheet in your Drive and syncs expenses and income there.

Can it read UPI and bank SMS?

Yes. Grant SMS permission and Kharch Log detects common Indian debit/credit messages. Unclear ones wait in the review inbox.

Do I need SMS permission?

Yes — SMS permission is required for bank and UPI auto-capture. That is how Kharch Log reads payment alerts on your phone.

Is my expense data private?

We don’t store your expense ledger on a Kharch Log server — entries sync directly to your Google Sheet. See the Privacy Policy for Google Sign-In and SMS details. SMS is read on-device for auto-capture.

Not yet. Android first. Your Google Sheet will still open on iPhone via the Sheets app if someone shares it with you.
