Free expense tracker FAQ

Is Kharch Log really free?
Yes — completely free expense tracker app. No ads, no paid plans.
Do I need Excel or special software?
No. Expenses sync to Google Sheets (free with any Google account). Open on phone, web, or export anytime.
Does it work for UPI and Indian banks?
Yes. Built for India — rupee amounts, UPI/bank SMS parsing, and categories suited to daily kharcha tracking.
Can I use it without SMS permission?
Absolutely. Manual entry works fully. SMS is optional for automatic expense logging.
How is this different from other budget apps?
Most apps lock data on their servers. Kharch Log writes to your Google Sheet — you own and export your expense history forever.
