See today first, trends second

Personal finance apps like Goodbudget and Monarch lead with dashboards — daily awareness plus category breakdowns. Kharch Log keeps it practical: a Today view for “what did I spend right now?” and reports when you want to zoom out to food, travel, or bills.

How reports & Today view work

  1. Today dashboardSee same-day debits and credits at a glance — useful after a shopping run or bill-payment day.
  2. Filter by date & categoryDrill into a week, month, or custom range; slice by category to spot leaks.
  3. Continue in SheetsExport-ready rows in Google Sheets mean you can build pivot tables and charts beyond what the app shows.

What you can learn from your data

Daily discipline

Today view nudges same-day logging — the habit that makes monthly reviews honest.

Category leaks

Food, transport, and subscriptions stand out when you filter by tag over 30 days.

Income vs spend

Money-in rows beside expenses give a clearer picture than debits alone.

No paywall charts

Core reports are free; heavy analysis can always continue in your Sheet.

Common questions

Do reports need SMS capture?

No — any logged expense (manual, voice, or approved SMS) appears in reports.

Can I see reports on desktop?

Yes — open the synced Google Sheet in a browser for charts and pivots.

How often does data update?

Reports reflect entries as soon as they sync to your Sheet.
