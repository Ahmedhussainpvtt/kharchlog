From logged expense to shareable proof

Freelancers, field staff, and salaried employees often need to send a transaction summary or receipt-style PDF for reimbursement. Kharch Log turns an entry you already logged into a share card or branded invoice PDF — without retyping details into Word or email.

How share & invoice PDF works

  1. Open transaction detailTap any expense to see amount, category, payee, date, and notes.
  2. Share or save PDFSend a formatted summary via WhatsApp, email, or save an invoice-style PDF to Files.
  3. Sheet stays authoritativeThe share is a snapshot; your Google Sheet remains the source of truth for totals.

When sharing helps

Reimbursements

Attach a clean PDF when submitting travel or client expenses to HR.

Split reminders

Share a transaction card when splitting dinner or trip costs with friends.

Record keeping

Save PDFs locally for warranty claims or tax supporting docs.

Promote the app

Share cards include Kharch Log branding — handy if you recommend the tracker to others.

Common questions

Is the PDF a tax invoice?

It is a transaction summary for personal records and reimbursement — not a GST tax invoice from a merchant.

Can I share before SMS is approved?

Share actions are available on saved transactions — approve pending SMS items first if needed.

Does sharing expose my whole Sheet?

No — only the single transaction you share is included in the card or PDF.
