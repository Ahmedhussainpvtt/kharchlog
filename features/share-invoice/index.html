Common questions

Does sharing expose my whole Sheet?

No - only the one transaction you choose.

Can I share income entries?

Yes - any saved transaction with share actions available.

Where is the PDF saved?

To your device Downloads/Files - you choose where to send it next.

Does the share include my phone number?

Only fields from the expense entry - not your SMS or Google account details.
