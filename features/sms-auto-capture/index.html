Bank & UPI SMS auto-capture

SMS permission powers automatic capture from debit and credit alerts. Prefer privacy? Skip SMS and log manually — currency is just a number.

  • ✓Parse common bank and UPI debit/credit messages on-device
  • ✓Send unclear SMS to the needs-review inbox
  • ✓No Kharch Log server stores your SMS — matched rows go to your Sheet

Automatic expense tracking without a bank login

Unlike Monarch-style bank sync, Kharch Log does not connect to your bank account. It optionally reads payment SMS already on your phone — similar in spirit to “track spending from the notifications you already get.” Works with any currency because the app logs amounts, not FX rates.
