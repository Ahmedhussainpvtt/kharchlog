Automatic expense tracking without a bank login

Many finance apps ask for net-banking credentials or paid bank sync. Kharch Log takes a different path: it reads payment SMS already on your Android phone — the same debit alerts from UPI, cards, and banks you get after every tap or swipe. Parsing happens on-device; matched rows sync to your Google Sheet.

How SMS auto-capture works

  1. Grant SMS permissionOnly needed if you want automatic capture. Skip it and log manually — the app still works.
  2. New payment SMS arrivesKharch Log listens for common bank/UPI debit and credit patterns — amount, merchant, date extracted locally.
  3. Clear match → SheetHigh-confidence parses become expense rows. Unclear messages go to the review inbox instead of silently logging wrong data.

Why SMS-based tracking works in India

UPI & card alerts

PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, and bank SMS formats are common — no need to open each app to copy amounts.

On-device parsing

SMS text is processed on your phone. Kharch Log does not upload message content to its own servers.

Your Sheet, your data

Like ExpenseTracker.io and Chat2Sheet, the ledger lives in Google Sheets you control — export, pivot, share.

Privacy choice

No SMS permission? Use manual or voice entry only. You stay in control of what the app can read.

Common questions

Is SMS permission required?

Only for automatic capture. Manual and voice entry work without it.

Does Kharch Log store my SMS?

No server-side SMS vault. Parsed expenses sync to your Google Sheet; unclear items wait in the review inbox on your device.

Will every bank SMS parse perfectly?

Not always — that is why the review inbox exists. You approve anything the parser is unsure about.
