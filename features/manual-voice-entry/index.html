Why fast expense entry matters

Most people quit expense tracking because logging feels like homework. Apps like Fast Budget and Voice Sheet prove the fix: capture in seconds, confirm once, move on. Kharch Log combines a focused manual form with voice add so you can log while walking, driving, or finishing a purchase — then every row lands in your Google Sheet.

How manual & voice entry works

  1. Open add expenseEnter amount, pick category, payment mode, payee, and optional notes. Toggle money in vs money out on the same screen.
  2. Or tap voice addSay something natural — “coffee sixty rupees” or “received salary fifty thousand”. The app parses amount and hints, then shows a confirm screen.
  3. Save to your SheetApproved entries sync to the Kharch Log spreadsheet in your Google Drive. Edit later from the app or directly in Sheets.

Built for real-life spending

Same-day accuracy

Log at the point of spend instead of reconstructing the week on Sunday. Daily trackers work when capture takes under ten seconds.

Income + expenses

Salary credits, refunds, and side income sit beside debits — one ledger, not two apps.

Any currency

Amount is just a number. Use ₹, $, €, or anything else — Kharch Log does not force a single currency format.

Hands-free moments

Voice entry mirrors how ChatBuk and Voice Sheet handle natural speech — speak, review, save.

Common questions

Do I have to use voice?

No. Manual entry is fully supported. Voice is optional when typing is inconvenient.

Can I edit after saving?

Yes — update entries from the transaction list or edit rows directly in Google Sheets.

Does voice work offline?

Voice recognition needs a connection on most devices; manual entry works anytime.
