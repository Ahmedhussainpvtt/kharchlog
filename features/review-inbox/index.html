Needs-review inbox for unclear SMS

Automation should never silently invent spending. Unclear messages wait for your decision — approve, reject, or edit first.

  • ✓See pending SMS-detected transactions in one inbox
  • ✓Approve clean matches; reject junk or duplicates

Human-in-the-loop expense control

Enterprise tools like Kredmint use approval workflows so finance teams catch mistakes before reimbursement. Kharch Log brings a lightweight version to personal tracking: auto-detected SMS never silently becomes a permanent expense until you say so.

How the review inbox works

  1. SMS parser flags uncertaintyPromotional texts, OTPs, balance enquiries, and ambiguous debits stay out of your Sheet.
  2. Review pending itemsOpen the inbox to see amount, payee hint, and original SMS snippet side by side.
  3. Approve, reject, or editOne tap to accept a clean match; reject junk; or fix category and amount before sync.

Why a review step matters

No phantom expenses

Failed parses do not inflate your monthly total — you decide what counts.

Duplicate protection

Reject repeats when the same UPI alert was already logged manually.

Category cleanup

Fix “Swiggy” vs “Food” before the row hits Sheets — reports stay accurate.

Trust in automation

Automation saves time; approval keeps you confident in the numbers.

Common questions

Can I approve from the transaction detail screen?

Yes — pending SMS-detected items show approve and reject actions until you decide.

What happens to rejected SMS?

They are dismissed and not written to your Google Sheet.

Do manual entries use the inbox?

No — only auto-captured SMS that need confirmation appear there.
