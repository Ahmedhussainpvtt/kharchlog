Your spreadsheet is the system of record

Closed budget apps lock history inside their platform. Kharch Log follows the Voice Sheet model: your Google Sheet is the database. The Android app is a fast capture layer; Sheets is where you pivot, chart, share with family, or hand off to an accountant.

How Google Sheets sync works

  1. Sign in with GoogleOne-time setup links Kharch Log to your Google account.
  2. Auto-create folder & sheetThe app creates a Kharch Log folder in Drive and a spreadsheet with structured columns for expenses.
  3. Every save syncsManual, voice, and approved SMS entries append as rows — open the same Sheet on web or desktop anytime.

Why Sheets beats a closed app vault

You own the file

Export CSV, duplicate the Sheet, or stop using the app — your history stays in Drive.

Limited scope

Kharch Log uses drive.file access — only files the app creates for you, not your entire Drive.

Household sharing

Share the Sheet with a partner; both see the same live ledger without a “family plan” upsell.

Excel-friendly

Download as .xlsx anytime — great if you outgrow the app but want the same data model.

Common questions

Can I use my existing spreadsheet?

The app creates a dedicated Kharch Log sheet by default. You can still copy rows into any workbook you already use.

Does Kharch Log read my other Drive files?

No — only the spreadsheet and folder created for Kharch Log under the limited scope.

What if I delete the app?

Your Google Sheet remains in Drive with every row you synced.
